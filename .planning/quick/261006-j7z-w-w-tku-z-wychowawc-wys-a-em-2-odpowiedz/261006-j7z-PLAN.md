<objective>
Fix missing sent replies in the message thread view (reported on the thread with educator "Sobota Łukasz", subject "składka 21 zł na maturę próbną z Operonem").

Purpose: Three compounding bugs caused sent replies to disappear:
1. `FirestoreMessagesDataSource.sendMessage()` only called `/api/sendMessage` (without Cloud Functions URL fallback) and `mockFallback.sendMessage()` without saving replies to `SchoolDataCacheManager` or Firestore, and `FirestoreMessagesDataSource.getMessages()` constructed `MessageThread` without passing `messages:`, so `MessageThread` always defaulted to a 1-item list containing only the teacher's initial message.
2. `MessageReplyComposer._handleSendReply()` immediately called `ref.invalidate(messagesProvider)`, which caused `app_router.dart` / `MessagesScreen` to rebuild with the 1-item `MessageThread` from `getMessages()`, wiping the optimistic reply state. It also hardcoded the student's name and role (`'Uczeń'`) instead of using the logged-in parent/user identity (`appUserProvider`).
3. On the backend, `LibrusClient.sendMessage()` was a stub that never submitted the Librus reply form (`/wiadomosci/3/5/:id`), `LibrusClient.fetchMessages()` did not scrape `/wiadomosci/6` (Wysłane) to attach sent replies to their parent threads, `exports.sendMessage` in `functions/index.js` did not persist the reply to `students/{login}` in Firestore, and `mergeAndIndexMessages` in `functions/src/sync_service.js` dropped `replies` during sync cycles.

Output: End-to-end persistence, Librus `/wiadomosci/6` correlation, cache hydration, and UI rendering of thread replies across mobile/web sessions and sync cycles.
</objective>
