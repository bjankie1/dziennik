<objective>
Enrich Telegram notifications for new Librus messages (`type: "message"`) and school announcements (`type: "announcement"`) so they include the full multiline content (`D-01`), safe word/line-boundary truncation within Telegram Bot API's 4096-character HTML limit (`D-02`), guaranteed pre-notification message detail/attachment hydration and multiline HTML extraction (`D-03`), clickable attachment download links pointing to `/api/downloadAttachment?path=...` without binary uploads (`D-04`, `D-05`), distinct visual templates with thread deep links (`D-06`), and compact `body` preservation for the in-app "Ostatnie alerty" list alongside enriched `content`, `messageId`, and `attachmentFiles` fields (`D-07`).

Purpose: Parents and students paired with the EduSync Telegram Bot can immediately read the complete content of new messages and school announcements and download any attached files directly from Telegram without opening Librus Synergia.
Output: Updated `functions/src/librus_client.js`, `functions/src/sync_service.js`, `functions/src/telegram_service.js`, and unit test suites in `functions/test/`.
