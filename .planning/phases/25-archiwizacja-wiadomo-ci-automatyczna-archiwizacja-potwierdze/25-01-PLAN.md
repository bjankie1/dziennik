         - Also detect newly accepted justifications by comparing `prevData.attendance` / `prevData.justifications` with `freshData.attendance` / `freshData.justifications`:
           - When attendance records for a date `YYYY-MM-DD` transition from `unexcused`/`absent`/`requested` in `prevData` to `excused` in `freshData` (and no `just_msg_` notification was already emitted in this sync run for that date), emit a `type: "justification"` notification (`id: just_att_${date}`, `title: "Zaakceptowano usprawiedliwienie (${date})"`, `body: "Usprawiedliwiono ${count} godz. lekcyjnych: ${subjects}"`).
       - Update `unreadMessagesCount` calculation on `studentRef.set` to exclude archived messages: `(freshData.messages || []).filter(m => !m.isRead && !m.isArchived).length`.
